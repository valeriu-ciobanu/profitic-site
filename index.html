Getting started

Frequently asked questions

How long does onboarding take?

You can start scoring ideas and tracking active projects on day one, without professional services or a months-long implementation. Within a week, you can have a complete view of your product portfolio.

How long is the free trial?

You can try Profitic free for 30 days.

Where do most teams start?

Most teams import their existing ideas and agree on a few criteria, such as strategic fit, customer demand, and effort. They score each idea against those criteria to decide which ones to explore first.

Do we need a financial model to get started?

No. Start with an idea or an active project. When you’re ready to assess the return, give Profitic AI your estimates for sales volume, price, and costs, then test how the outcome changes.

How does Profitic work with our product development tools?

Profitic is the strategic alignment layer across teams. It gives you one place to agree on what to build, what return to expect, and what it will take to launch. Teams do the work in their existing tools, like Jira or Azure DevOps. Profitic AI brings milestone updates back into the plan so everyone can see what’s on track and where a delay could affect growth.

What can we share with the board?

Show which investments support each growth initiative, their forecast contribution, and how that compares with the target. If a launch slips, you can explain what changed and which decision needs leadership’s attention.
